ThunderMax Tuning – Comprehensive 50 Page Guide
Installation & Setup
- Install ThunderMax ECM in place of stock unit. - Replace narrow-band O2 with wide-band O2 sensors
(18mm bungs if required). - Use dielectric grease on connectors. - Initialize ECM: Ignition ON 20s →
OFF 5s → Start, idle 15s. - Clear diagnostic codes and Learned Fuel Adjustments after flashing.

Ignition Timing Strategy
- Idle: 16–18° advance, stable across 768–1280 RPM. - Light Cruise: Gradual ramp-up with RPM for
efficiency. - Midrange (2200–3200 RPM): Critical torque band; richen AFR, retard rear jug 1–2°. - WOT:
Hold advance only if no pinging.

AutoTune Management
- Use AutoTune in 1500–4000 RPM range first. - Lock stable zones (Idle, WOT, Decel) to prevent drift.
- Save session data and review VE correction heatmaps before writing. - Disable AutoTune in proven
regions to prevent over-learning.

Advanced Strategies
- Rear Cylinder Thermal Tuning: Retard timing by 1–2° above 2200 RPM to control heat. - Multi-Mode
Profiles: • Street: AFR 13.6 cruise, conservative timing. • Race: Leaner cruise, aggressive spark, WOT
at 12.8 AFR. • Heat: Richer AFR + retarded timing above 230°F. - Fuel Phase Smoothing: Blend VE
transitions to avoid harsh throttle stutters. - VE Table Polishing: Focus corrections in torque band, lock
stable regions.
