1. Base Setup & Idle RPM
Correct ECU pairing, firmware stability, TPS calibration.
■■ WARNING: Never tune with low battery or O■ faults present.

Throttle Logic – ThunderMax Master Tuning Reference • Print Edition v1.0 • Aug 2025 | Page 4

2. AFR Strategy
Targets: Idle 13.6–13.8, Cruise 13.2–13.4, Accel 12.8–13.0, WOT 12.6–12.8.
Rear jug bias +0.3 richer.
■■ WARNING: Never run leaner than 13.4 under load on 131ci.

Throttle Logic – ThunderMax Master Tuning Reference • Print Edition v1.0 • Aug 2025 | Page 5

3. Spark Tables & Timing
Front: Idle 12–14°, Cruise 28–30°, WOT 26–28°. Rear offset –3°.
■■ WARNING: Never exceed 32° under load.

Throttle Logic – ThunderMax Master Tuning Reference • Print Edition v1.0 • Aug 2025 | Page 6

4. VE Adjustments
Smooth learned trims into VE. ±3–6% edits max.
Lock stable regions to prevent drift.
■■ WARNING: Don’t chase unstable trims without fixing O■/exhaust.

Throttle Logic – ThunderMax Master Tuning Reference • Print Edition v1.0 • Aug 2025 | Page 7

5. AutoTune Management
Short 20–30 min loops by band (cruise, accel, WOT).
Lock zones, blend trims, validate with learning OFF.
■■ WARNING: Long mixed rides produce noisy data.

Throttle Logic – ThunderMax Master Tuning Reference • Print Edition v1.0 • Aug 2025 | Page 8

6. Rear Cylinder Timing
Rear jug runs hotter. Offset –2° to –4° in cruise, +0.3 richer AFR.
■■ WARNING: Don’t advance rear jug timing to front levels in hot zones.

Throttle Logic – ThunderMax Master Tuning Reference • Print Edition v1.0 • Aug 2025 | Page 9

7. Timing vs Engine Temp & RPM
Use timing retard vs temp tables to prevent knock above 320°F.
■■ WARNING: Too much retard = lazy throttle, hot exhaust.

Throttle Logic – ThunderMax Master Tuning Reference • Print Edition v1.0 • Aug 2025 | Page 10

8. Diagnostics & Common Fixes
Check O■, intake leaks, battery voltage before tuning.
■■ WARNING: Don’t tune around broken hardware.
