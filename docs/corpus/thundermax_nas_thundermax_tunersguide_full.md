Chapter 1 – Foundations
Chapter 1 – Foundations
ThunderMax is not flash-and-forget. It’s base maps + AutoTune offsets. The tune lives in the base map, not the

Offsets under 10%: okay. 10–20%: AutoMap. 20%+: wrong map or hardware issue.

Loop: Base Map → Ride → Offsets → AutoMap → Updated Base Map.
Chapter 2 – Fuel Control
Chapter 2 – Fuel Control
AFR targets shape everything.

Idle/low load: 13.8–14.2 AFR
Cruise: 13.6–14.0 AFR
WOT: 12.5–13.0 AFR

Rear trims look ugly but that’s normal. Trust sensors.
Lean for cruise, rich for load. Heat map: cruise AFR 14.2+, WOT 12.5 AFR.
Chapter 3 – Ignition Control
Chapter 3 – Ignition Control
Timing makes or breaks it.

Light throttle: smooth, no ping.
Cruise: rarely pings.
Roll-On: 95% of ping (the Belly).
WOT: timing can advance again.

Ping fixes: light = -2°, moderate = -3°, heavy = -5°, severe = -7°.
Timing vs Temp: pull ~1° per 4°F above 226°F.
Rear Cylinder Offset: -2° to -4°.
Chapter 4 – Idle & Rideability
Chapter 4 – Idle & Rideability
Idle speed: 1050 cold taper to 950 warm.
Throttle response: 40–60% smooths snatch, 80–100% sharp.
Decel fuel cut: off = no pop. Enrich 0–2% TPS cells if needed.
Cranking fuel: +5–10% if hard starts.
Chapter 5 – Advanced Control
Chapter 5 – Advanced Control
Knock Control: frequent hits = pull 2° timing.
VVT: cam timing boosts midrange torque on M8 modules.
Boost: retard ~1.5° per psi. Conservative saves pistons.
Auto Support/MLog: compare commanded vs actual AFR. Fix exhaust leaks/sensor delay if they don’t match.
Chapter 6 – Profiles & Recipes
Chapter 6 – Profiles & Recipes
Race Mode: Cruise AFR 13.6, WOT 12.5, aggressive spark, 1000 idle.
Street Mode: Cruise AFR 14.0, WOT 12.8, moderate spark, 950 idle.
Heat/Range Mode: Cruise AFR 14.2+, spark -2° above 280°F, idle 900–925, max MPG & cooling.
Chapter 7 – Troubleshooting
Chapter 7 – Troubleshooting
Ping: roll-on only = Belly. WOT = advance. Heat ping = Timing vs Temp.
Overheating: too lean cruise, weak spark trim, bad rear trim.
Decel Pop: disable fuel cut, fatten decel AFR, check leaks.
Bad AFR tracking: dirty O2, leaks, wrong injectors.
