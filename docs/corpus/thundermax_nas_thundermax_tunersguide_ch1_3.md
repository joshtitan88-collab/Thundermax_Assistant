Rule #1: The tune isn’t the offsets. AutoTune just shows you where the map is wrong.

Offsets under 10%: OK.

Chapter 2 – Fuel Control
Chapter 2 – Fuel Control
Getting AFR right before you ever touch spark.

ThunderMax follows AFR targets in the AFR vs TPS @ RPM maps.

Idle/low load: 13.8–14.2 AFR
Cruise: 13.6–14.0 AFR
WOT: 12.5–13.0 AFR

Rear cylinder trims always look messy—trust the sensors.

Offsets under 10%: ignore.
10–20%: AutoMap.
20%+: wrong base map or injector mismatch.

Balance: Lean for cruise, rich for load. Example:
Cruise 14.2+ AFR, WOT 12.5 AFR, blended for MPG + power.
Chapter 3 – Ignition Control
Chapter 3 – Ignition Control
Where horsepower is made and motors are lost.

Timing vs TPS @ RPM defines spark:
- Light throttle: smooth, no ping.
- Cruise: rarely pings.
- Roll-On: 95% of ping occurs here (the Belly).
- WOT: can advance again.

Adjustments:
Light ping: -1 to -2°
Moderate ping: -3°
Heavy ping: -5°
Severe ping: -7 to -8°

Timing vs Engine Temp: start pulling timing at 226°F, about 1° per 4°F rise.

Rear Cylinder Offset: -2° to -4° typical to balance heat load.

Ping happens in the belly—fix it there first.
